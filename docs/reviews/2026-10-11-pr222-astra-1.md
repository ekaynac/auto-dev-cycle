## Review of #222 (Astra, 2026-10-11)

Verdict: NO-GO

1. **High — Durable liability accounting is incorrectly marked as already enforced.**  
   `docs/design/m1/01-invariant-inventory.md:102` marks settlement liabilities **kept**, but `internal/lease/lease.go:settlement` only checks that settled calls and liabilities are disjoint. More decisively, `internal/capacity/reserve.go:329` refuses settlement with uncertain liabilities because the group ledger cannot hold their amounts yet. `ledger.Reconcile` does not implement that transfer. This leaves the interrupted-call path either stranded or dependent on dropping its liability; P16’s “kept liability” is insufficiently specified.

   **Fix:** mark this GAP and assign a bounded accounting PR before live calls activate. Specify durable call-to-lease/group binding, reservation-to-liability transfer, the positive-reservation fallback for calls without measurements, late usage after settlement, reconciliation and replay. Require fault evidence that available capacity decreases exactly once and uncertain usage survives restart.

2. **High — X3 requires an impossible recovery transition.**  
   `docs/design/m1/00-slice-plan.md:126` requires A1 to become `completed`, followed by A2 superseding A1 with the next token. `internal/lease/attempt.go:96` permits supersession only from `sealed`; completed attempts are terminal. Starting another first attempt on the same unit is also refused by `lease.Projector.start`. Thus the pinned crash exit cannot pass while preserving the existing contract.

   **Fix:** specify a valid recovery sequence, preferably `lost → sealed → superseded`, with replacement allocation atomic and the old lease’s settlement explicitly described. Also pin a barrier for the crash and an actual late-output injection: “any A1 output” is vacuous if the killed adapter never submits again. Compare total accounted usage—settled calls plus outstanding liabilities—with served usage; liabilities alone need not equal reconciled charges.

3. **High — The sequence activates work before restart safety is available.**  
   `docs/design/m1/00-slice-plan.md:258` introduces the runnable task driver, while P18 supplies recovery afterward. The dependencies at lines 278–280 permit P17 without P16, and permit P19’s X3 acceptance before P18. A crash in that intermediate installed product leaves running attempts without the promised recovery path. This conflicts with D46’s prohibition on activating a known blocking defect.

   **Fix:** land the recovery and reconciliation prerequisites before enabling task execution, or keep execution explicitly guarded until an activation PR after P18. Make P19 depend on P18. Connect P9 to the build/gate dependency chain and P5 to activation; both currently have no downstream dependency ensuring their completion.
