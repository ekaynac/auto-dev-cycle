# PR #56 (proposals and revalidation): Astra reviews

## Review of #56 (Astra, 2026-10-05)

Verdict: NO-GO

1. **High — Apply-time revalidation accepts a different binding.**  
   [internal/authority/proposal.go:219](/tmp/adc-pr56-astra-eWjjZx/internal/authority/proposal.go:219) checks proposer and action, but never compares the project or re-decided classes with the proposal. It also never recomputes `BindingDigest`.

   Reproduced: a confirmed proposal passes `Revalidate` after changing the request’s project under a grant covering both projects, or replacing `policy.widen_destinations` with `policy.widen_data` while retaining human policy. Changing the proposal’s scope or object while retaining its old digest also passes. Confirmation therefore does not reliably bind the operation accepted at apply.

   **Fix:** compare the complete operation being applied against the confirmed binding, including project, object, scope and normalized effective classes; recompute and verify the digest. Invalidate every mismatch, even when grant and confirmation policy remain unchanged.

2. **High — The confirmation transition bypasses expiry and confirmation checks.**  
   [internal/authority/proposal.go:116](/tmp/adc-pr56-astra-eWjjZx/internal/authority/proposal.go:116) permits `Close(p, confirmed)` for any proposal whose status is `open`, without checking its expiry or either confirmation gate. [Revalidate:213](/tmp/adc-pr56-astra-eWjjZx/internal/authority/proposal.go:213) then trusts that status.

   Reproduced: an already-expired open proposal becomes confirmed through `Close` and passes apply-time revalidation without a consumed challenge or review receipt. The expiry checks in `ConsumeChallenge` and `ConfirmByReview` do not protect this transition.

   **Fix:** exclude `confirmed` from generic `Close`. Provide checked confirmation transitions that enforce open/unexpired status and the required evidence together. Human confirmation must incorporate the dashboard’s verified WebAuthn result and atomically consume the challenge.

3. **Medium — The lifecycle cannot record required apply-time invalidation through `Close`.**  
   [internal/authority/proposal.go:117](/tmp/adc-pr56-astra-eWjjZx/internal/authority/proposal.go:117) rejects `confirmed → invalidated`. `Revalidate` only returns an error, leaving the supplied record confirmed. Its stale-state branch also returns `stale_expected_state`, contrary to the PR body’s claim that every change returns `proposal_invalidated`.

   **Fix:** provide an explicit terminal invalidation transition and require the writer to persist it for every failed revalidation, including stale state. Test that restoring authority later cannot make an invalidated proposal applicable.

4. **Medium — Stored classes do not use the digest’s canonical representation.**  
   [internal/authority/proposal.go:94](/tmp/adc-pr56-astra-eWjjZx/internal/authority/proposal.go:94) stores the original class list, although `BindingDigest` sorts and deduplicates it. Reproduced: duplicate decision classes produce a successful `NewProposal` whose record fails the proposal schema’s `uniqueItems` constraint. Unsorted classes also make the stored field order differ from the array actually hashed.

   **Fix:** normalize classes once and use the same sorted, unique list in both the record and digest.

Only findings **1–2 block**.

The PR’s two contract choices are acceptable with explicit persistence and provenance requirements:

- The original grant may be supplied separately, but its identity must be **durably associated with the proposal when created**, survive restart, and never be reconstructed by selecting a currently covering grant. Keeping it only in memory until an eventual approval record is insufficient.
- Caller-supplied excluded families must include the proposer and **every** contributor, derived from authoritative provenance. Missing provenance must produce an unknown-family refusal, not omission. Likewise, apply-time human revalidation must load the actual confirming actor from persisted confirmation evidence.

The checked challenge path rejects reuse, expiry, foreign proposal/binding, and unauthorized confirmers. Closed proposals cannot reopen. The four reasons and generated constants are consistent. Dashboard-only WebAuthn verification and transactional persistence remain integration obligations for the forthcoming hook.

### Lead notes on round 1 (applied)

The review was of 517c252; 7f25215 (from the automatic security review) already put the grant on the proposal record
and checked the project and classes. The round-1 commit completes the fixes:

- High 1 (binding at apply): `Revalidate` takes the operation being applied (its object and scope) and requires the
  request's actor, action, project, re-decided classes, the object, the proposal's expected state and the scope to
  hash to the proposal's binding digest; the proposal record must also hash to its own digest (a record's scope or
  classes changed under it is invalidated). The separate proposer/action check became redundant and was dropped.
- High 2 (confirmation path): `Close` only rejects, expires or supersedes. A proposal is confirmed only by
  `ConfirmHuman` (open, unexpired, human policy, the dashboard's verified `Assertion` for that challenge and confirmer,
  and the challenge consumed in the same step) or by `ConfirmByReview` (which now returns the confirmed proposal).
  Consuming a challenge alone is no longer exported.
- Medium 3 (invalidation): `Invalidate` ends an open or confirmed proposal for good; the writer persists it on every
  failed revalidation; an invalidated proposal is refused at apply even after authority is restored. A moved object
  keeps `stale_expected_state` (the stale exit code, 08); the PR body now says so.
- Medium 4 (canonical classes): one sorted, unique class list feeds both the digest and the record; repeated decision
  classes give a schema-valid proposal.
- Contract notes: the grant is now durably on the proposal record (7f25215); `excluded` families must come from
  authoritative provenance with unknowns passed as "unknown"; the human confirmer at apply comes from the persisted
  confirmation evidence. These are the hook PR's obligations.
