### Medium

**M1. The start check and placement disagree on what one call needs, so a priced lease can still be below one call's floor.**
`internal/registry/registry.go:180` requires `context_tokens × expected_turns ≥ floor`. Placement (`internal/dispatch/reserve.go:62`) admits a priced lease whenever the rails' fit buys one *turn* (`context_tokens`) and reserves `min(fit, attempt_cap)`. Input: the stub builder (64,000 × 2) passes the check; a day rail with 384,000 micros left at $4/M places a lease of 96,000 tokens (place_test's own row), whose first request floors at 128,000 output tokens plus its prompt. At a one-turn fit the overshoot is ≥ 64,000 tokens. The reviewer is the same in another form: it runs one turn whatever `expected_turns` says (run.go:325), yet 36,000 × 2 passes while its one turn's context is half the floor. The comment "a lease of it could never cover one request's floor" is true only for unrailed executors. Bounded by one attempt and visible at the next placement, so Medium. Fix: one unit for both rules. Smallest: `CheckFloors` compares `context_tokens` (one turn) against the floor, since the rails can shrink a lease to one turn; adjust the two fixture priors. Or: placement's "less than one turn" compares fit against `max(context_tokens, floor[role])` with the floors handed to the Dispatcher. State the remaining residual either way.

**M2. The reviewer's floor counts the prompt's raw bytes; the gateway counts the JSON-encoded request body.**
`internal/daemon/run.go:129` floors at `Tokens(review.MaxPromptBytes)`; the gateway floors `Tokens(len(body))` (`internal/gateway/count.go:83`), where the body is the adapter's whole request: the prompt JSON-escaped (every quote, backslash and newline doubled; other control characters six bytes, and `review.Prompt` admits any valid UTF-8, `internal/review/prompt.go:115`), the system message carrying the schema, and `response_format` carrying it again. Input: a 512 KiB diff of U+0001 encodes to 3 MiB, about 196,000 input tokens against the stated ~38,950; an ordinary diff with 5% quotes and newlines adds ~1,600 tokens, past the stub reviewer's margin (72,000 against a floor of about 70,950). `TestTheReviewersFloorCoversItsPrompt` measures the prompt, not the body, so it cannot catch this. Bounded by one call and by `maxRequestBytes` (8 MiB, 524,288 tokens). Fix: derive the floor from the encoded body (the prompt at its bounds inside a message, as `JSON.stringify` renders it, plus the schema twice), and name the control-character case as a residual next to the backtick-fence one in the `MaxPromptBytes` comment (`prompt.go:41-47`).
