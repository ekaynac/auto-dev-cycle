### High

**H1. The I18 check does not bind the brief's bytes, so an acceptance can ride in a "brief".** This fails judge items 1 and 2.
- `intent.briefOf` (`internal/intent/intent.go:159-176`) calls `brief.Check`. `brief.Check` (`internal/brief/brief.go:52-63`) decodes the blob against the schema and compares only `task` and `spec_digest`, at `brief.go:58`.
- It never compares the title or the requirement, or the bytes, with `brief.Of(task)`. So the promise "the bytes are the task's own brief" does not hold, and the encoding is not enforced as canonical.
- **Probe:** a variant of `TestAForgedPlanBriefIsRefused`, with the plan result recorded by hand. Two forgeries were **accepted**: `intent.Builder` returned nil, and the ref was in the build's rendered context.
  1. A brief with the right `task` and `spec_digest` and `requirement: "CANARY-ACCEPTANCE: func TestHidden…"`. This is a channel for the acceptance test source to reach the builder, which is exactly what I18 forbids. The `hidden` digest check misses it, because the digest is the brief's and not the acceptance blob's.
  2. The task's own brief with one leading space: other bytes under another id, root, `ID == sha256(bytes)`. This is the "same content under another id" case.
- **Fix:**
  - In `intent.Load`, compute `_, own, err := brief.Of(authority.TaskOf(tx, task))`. In `briefOf`, require `rec.ContentDigest == own`. Keep the root and blob checks; the blob read becomes only an integrity check.
  - Since `own` binds the task and the spec digest, `brief.Check`'s field comparison and `authority.TaskSpecDigest` become redundant. Delete them, or keep `Check` only for the planner's self-test.
  - I applied this fix in the copy, and it refused both forgeries.
  - Add both forgeries as rows of `TestAForgedPlanBriefIsRefused`, plus the mutation "the brief's content unchecked".

### Judged and found sound
- **Another task's material:**
  - `graph.Records` lists only the task's own results, and the controller's extras get no `plan` producer;
  - an id recorded by two producers is ambiguous, so it does not resolve;
  - another task's or another spec's brief is refused.
- **Lineage and type:** a ref with a lineage, a non-blob, or `ID != content digest` is refused before any blob is read. The check runs in `resolve` before the declassified (`shallow`) shortcut, and is cached once per walk.
- **Bounds:**
  - each plan ref costs at most one 1 MiB blob read, cached;
  - the number of records is still bounded by `maxRecords`;
  - the planner writes one blob (about 20 KB at most, given the spec's limits) per claimed plan per pass;
  - the brief's title and requirement limits match `task-create.json` (200 and 20000), so `brief.Of` cannot fail on a valid spec.
- **The brief's content:**
  - it holds no acceptance names or digests;
  - `brief.Of`'s bytes are canonical (RFC 8785), with the digest over those bytes;
  - `Restriction`'s digest goes through `eventstore.ContentDigest`, which canonicalises.
- **Launch stays inactive:** Plan is decided only under `Gates.Evaluates`, and with no planner the action leaves the claimed plan untouched (`errNothing`).
