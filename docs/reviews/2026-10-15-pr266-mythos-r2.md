Input 2:


Trace. `pyPytest` allows `pytest.mark.NAME.with_args` (`:56`), so the decorator is an allowlisted chain plus one call of constants. `pySkipString` at the `skipif` token sees `t[i+1]` is `.`, not `(`, and continues; `with_args` is not a mark name.
