# PR #82 (live-record shapes, B5): Astra reviews

## Review of #82 (Astra, 2026-10-05)

Verdict: NO-GO

- **High — Killed-call reasons contradict exit-map precedence.** `docs/design/m0/11-testing.md:120–121`, repeated at `145–146`. A killed call that emitted `credit_exhausted`, or whose controller-observed status was 402, remains `could_not_run/broker_credit_exhausted`, even with `StopRequested` and `Protocol` both true. Likewise, rate-limit hints or observed 429 retain `quota_exhausted`. The new unconditional stop/protocol split loses capacity holds under ordinary operation. `internal/vocab/exitmap/exitmap.go:68–92` explicitly prioritises credit, then quota, then stop, then protocol.
  
  **Fix:** derive the reason from all exit-map inputs, including earlier error-event hints and controller-observed statuses. Restrict the stated stop/protocol alternatives to cases without capacity causes. Define stop by the recorded `attempt.stop_requested`, not confirmed termination. Require fixture variants covering capacity hints and observed 402/429, with and without a stop request.

- **High — “Sum of its usage deltas” makes the no-deltas case financially free.** `docs/design/m0/11-testing.md:122–123`. A call can incur charges and die before reporting any usage—the original B5 case explicitly includes “no usage.” Its empty delta sum is zero, contradicting both “never as free” and the reservation fallback specified in `schemas/adapter/run-result.json:54`. Note 05 requires the **best estimate** and subtracts uncertain liabilities from affordability. `ledger.Reconcile` only returns calls represented by measurements (`internal/capacity/ledger/ledger.go:97–141`); it cannot independently recover an unreported call or its reservation.
  
  **Fix:** specify the deduplicated delta sum when deltas exist, otherwise the call’s reservation, retained as an uncertain liability pending reconciliation. Extend the fixture at `145–147` to assert the amount and its effect on available capacity, including a positive reservation with zero measurements. Merely asserting an “uncertain” flag misses this failure.

- **Medium — Capture provenance and permissible transformations remain underspecified.** `docs/design/m0/11-testing.md:115–119`. “Captured instance” does not identify the capture boundary or evidence required to distinguish an actual emitted record from a manually constructed example. The fake-adapter alternative also does not explain how controller-owned dispositions and receipts are captured. `fixtures/legacy/README.md` already documents legacy sources and anonymisation, but the new rule does not extend that recordkeeping to every new capture.
  
  **Fix:** require per-capture metadata identifying the producer/version, source run or reproducible fake scenario, capture boundary, and anonymisation/trimming transformations. Preserve missing versus null fields, types, record relationships, and ordering. For the killed-call fixture, capture the interrupted stream and controller observations with the terminal record genuinely absent; assert controller-derived `completed: false`. That field is not an adapter run-result field, and a terminal result containing `usage_final: null` tests a different case.

Static contract review; no files modified.
### Lead notes on round 1 (applied)

- High (precedence): the killed-call reason is whatever the exit map derives from everything observed. A capacity cause from error-event hints or controller-observed statuses comes first (credit, then quota). Only without one is it `attempt_stopped` (on a recorded `stop_requested`, a request rather than confirmed termination) or `adapter_protocol_error`. The fixture covers 402 and 429, by hint and by observed status, with and without a stop request.
- High (no deltas): the liability is the best estimate: the deduplicated delta sum when deltas exist, otherwise the call's reservation. Available capacity drops by it. The fixture asserts the amount and its effect, with a positive reservation and no measurements.
- Medium (provenance): each capture is taken at a stated boundary (adapter stream, controller event log, ledger) and carries metadata: producer and version, source run or reproducing fake scenario, boundary, transformations. Transformations preserve missing versus null, types, relationships and order. The killed-call stream is captured with the terminal result genuinely absent, and `completed: false` is the controller's derivation.
