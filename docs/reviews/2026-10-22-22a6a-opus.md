### Answers to the four questions

**1. A review recorded with a roster that is not independent?** No review is claimed on one.
- **By design, `Record` records a roster that is not independent** (the first admitted reviewer) when no reviewer qualifies. `ClaimReview` then refuses it and holds it, so it is never claimed.
- **`reviews.independent` mirrors `graph.independentOf`:**
  - an unknown contributor family, including a build result with no lease, makes every reviewer not independent;
  - the reviewer's own family must be known.
- **A reviewer that is also a builder,** or of the builder's family, is skipped (row).
- **A contributor added after recording:** it is caught at the claim (`ClaimReview`), and at the commit (`reviewIndependent`, which is 6c's). Unheld runs a fresh check against the contributors each pass.

**2. The graph's review identity: intact.**
- The roster is in the params and the key; both nodes go through `graph.RecordReview`, which checks `registered`.
- The requirement is committed with its params as its conclusions (`recordsRequirement`). `bindsRequirement` and supersession are the graph's own and unchanged, and the fold refuses any replacement whose requirement is not the predecessor's revision + 1.

**3. Can the hold spin, never clear, or clear wrongly?**
- **Spin:** no. Unheld commits only on a change, and the clear path's check matches `ClaimReview`'s, so a clear cannot be followed at once by a fresh hold.
- **Wrong clear:** none found.
- **Never clear:** yes (H1).

**4. Paths already active:** unchanged.
- **`noRequirement`:** build, accept and gate keep exactly one kind each, with the same semantics.
- **`Observe`:**
  - its new completed-gate case catches nodes that matched no case before;
  - every new review case is behind `g.Evaluates && g.Reviews`;
  - a claimed review is excluded from `Granted`.
- **`placer.card`:** changes only for review nodes.

### High

**H1. The hold can become permanent, and the loop logs two refusals every pass. BLOCKING.** `internal/daemon/reviews.go:191-230`
- **Cause:** the new requirement revision is `max(revision) + 1` over every requirement node of the task, and supersession picks any requirement node whose roster equals the new one. Recording the next revision and superseding happen in separate passes, so there is a window between them. The design note says "both in one command".
  - The fold accepts only the predecessor's requirement revision + 1.
  - A revision recorded and then abandoned (an orphan) therefore sends the next one to + 2, which the fold refuses forever.
- **Input (probe):**
  1. The stub registry. Two passes record and commit `review_requirement` revision 1 and the review, on `stubReviewer` (family `stub-reviewer`).
  2. A restart with `stubReviewer`'s family changed to `fam-b`. Three passes:
     - the review is held;
     - revision 2 is recorded on `fam-b` and is still runnable.
  3. A restart with the family `fam-c`. Ten passes.
- **Result:**
  - **The review stays held,** permanently.
  - **Revision 3 is recorded on `fam-c`.** Every pass, `Supersede(review, rev3)` is refused: "the replacement's requirement is the next revision, of its subject, roster and definition version".
  - **Revision 2 is held, and revision 3 claimed.** Every pass, `Require(rev3)` is refused: "candidate is at requirement revision 1: only a supersession moves it".
  - **The log:** two alternating messages beat `Loop.refused`'s last-message deduplication, so both are logged on every pass, forever.
  - With one or two passes under `fam-b`, before revision 2 is recorded, the same sequence ends claimed on `fam-c`. The window is the passes between recording revision 2 and superseding onto it.
- **Fix:** compute the next revision from the held review's own requirement (`was.Revision + 1`), never `max`. Supersede only onto a requirement node at exactly that revision with the roster derived now.
  - **Best:** record that requirement and supersede in one command, as the design note has it.
  - **Or:** when the node at `was + 1` has another roster, record a fresh node at `was + 1`. `selectRevision` admits it, since that revision was never selected. Then leave orphans unclaimed: `Observe` should give `Require` only requirement nodes at the selected revision, or at the one a supersession moves to.
  - **A row:** two restarts with different families, restarting between the recording and the supersession; the replacement is claimed on the last roster.

### Medium

**M1. An orphaned requirement node is claimed and committed. OBLIGATION (6a′).** `internal/reconcile/reconcile.go` (`Requirements`), `internal/daemon/reviews.go:174`
- **Cause:** any claimed `review_requirement` of the task is given to `Require`, including one no review will ever be superseded onto.
- **Effect:** in H1's input, revision 3 is refused on every pass. Even after H1's fix, a revision orphaned by a second restart would keep failing `Require`, since its commit's selection is refused.
- **Fix:** decide `Require` only for the subject's selected revision, or for the requirement a held review is being superseded onto. Or invalidate orphans.

### Low

**L1. The review's plan and patch inputs come from the last non-superseded node of each kind, not from the gate's own chain. OBLIGATION.** `internal/daemon/reviews.go:83`
- **Cause:** with more than one build or plan node in a task (rounds or repairs, which come later), the last one in `TaskNodes` order is taken, whichever produced the candidate the completed gate checked.
- **Fix:** walk the gate's inputs back: gate → accept → build → plan. Name the producers from those inputs.

**L2. A claimed review keeps a roster the registry no longer derives, until 6c's commit. OBLIGATION (state it).**
- **Input (probe):** a restart flips the reviewer's family from `fam-b` back to `stub-reviewer` while the replacement review is claimed and leased.
- **Result:** the review stays claimed, with its lease on that reviewer. Only the commit's `registered` check refuses it (6c).
- **Fix:** state that a roster change after the claim is caught at the commit. Or decide `ReviewUnblock`-style re-derivation for claimed, unrun reviews too.
