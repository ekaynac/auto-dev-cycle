## Opus pre-review of #253

Verdict: NO-GO

One High finding blocks the merge. Settlement now depends on `ledger.Reconcile` succeeding for every bound call. A hostile adapter can make it fail, and that refuses the lease's settlement for good. Nothing in the design can clear it, not even a later provider reconciliation. The rest of the accounting holds:
- A lease has at most one attempt: the lease table allows `granted → active` only once, and supersession needs a new lease.
- Duplicate groups are refused at grant.
- The `liable` field is omitted at zero.
- Pricing never reads `amount_micros`.
- I reran M1 and M5. Both turn red as the PR states: M1 fails `TestUnmeasuredCallSettlesAsItsReservation`, and M5 fails `TestReportMatchesTheBinding/{another_lease,another_attempt}`.

I ran the probes below at 065bc88 using the PR's own `liability_test.go` helpers.

### High

**H1. A hostile adapter can permanently wedge a lease's settlement, and the group's reservation with it** (`internal/capacity/liability.go:73`, `:95`; reached from `settle` → `checkSettlement` → `reconciled` → `ledger.Reconcile`).
- **Cause:** `Reconcile` returns an error, rather than a figure, in two cases:
  - a call's reports name two currencies (`ledger.go:136`);
  - its delta sum passes `maxAmount` (`ledger.go:173`).
- **Who controls the inputs:** the adapter host (`adapterhost/attempt.go:331`, `submit.go:304-323`) copies the adapter's `usage` events as they are. Each delta is schema-valid on its own: currency matches `^[A-Z]{3}$` and every token count is ≤ 2^53−1. Before settlement, `ledger.Project` accepts them, because it does not reconcile.
- **Probe 1, currency:**
  - Recorded: delta seq 0 in USD, delta seq 1 in EUR, then a `provider_reconciliation` in USD.
  - `capacity.Settlement` fails with "reports in USD and EUR".
  - Settling the call in `calls` and in `liabilities` is refused both ways.
  - Outstanding stays at 400.
- **Probe 2, overflow:** two deltas of 2^53−1 input tokens. The settlement is refused ("usage overflows").
- **Who unblocks it: no one.** P20's provider reconciliation does not help, because `Reconcile` fails before it reaches the reconciliation branch. So:
  - the lease stays `settling`;
  - its reservation stays held on the group indefinitely;
  - X3's recovery sequence stops at step 4.
- **Introduced here:** before P12b, settlement never called `Reconcile`.
- **Same problem after settlement:** a late report in another currency on a liable call is refused at `lateUsage`. If P20 records its reconciliation in a different currency from the adapter's estimates, P20 can never record it.
- **Fix:** settlement and liability must not depend on a figure that can fail.
  1. In `reconciled`, treat a `Reconcile` error as "uncertain, unmeasured". The call then owes its positive reservation, or, better, the reservation and a saturated token sum. Mark it `inconsistent_usage`; do not return the error. Then settlement and `lateUsage` always go through.
  2. Separately, make the currency check token-agnostic. Token liability does not depend on currency, so the currency conflict should only flag USD pricing.
  3. Optionally, have the host drop or flag a report that would conflict, before `finish`.
- **Tests to add:** these two probes, plus a provider reconciliation after the conflict that releases the liability.

### Medium

**M1. Entry 62 is only half closed: every token figure is still the upstream's own report** (`ledger.go:78-104`; `adapterhost/submit.go:308` sets `source_of_truth: adapter_report`).
- **What #226 entry 62 asks:** price from the gateway's observed tokens.
- **What exists:** the gateway counts no tokens. `gateway.Observed` holds only statuses and refusals.
- **Probe 3:** liability 400. A 1-token delta takes it to 1. A `final` reporting 0 tokens then settles the call at 0.
- **Effect:** a hostile upstream can make any call cost nothing, both in capacity and in P17's USD rail.
- **The PR's rationale for "a late delta lowers the fallback" is wrong.** It says this keeps the result independent of report order. `max(reservation, delta sum)` is just as order-independent.
- **Fix:**
  - Do not claim entry 62 closed. Re-file the gateway-count half (`gateway_count`) with an owner (P11b or P30).
  - Ask the lead whether an uncertain call's liability should be `max(reservation, delta sum)`. That needs a matching change to X3(b) and to Q8's "delta sum, or the reservation when none".

**M2. A zero reservation can wedge a lease, and nothing upstream prevents it** (`liability.go:220`; `modelcall.go:120`).
- `Amount` has `minimum: 0`, and `modelcall.Intend` checks only that the lease has groups, not that the amounts are positive.
- So a call can bind to a lease whose reservation is 0. If the call is then unmeasured, the lease cannot settle until a report or a reconciliation arrives.
- In noquery mode neither ever arrives, which blocks P21 recovery.
- **Fix:** in `modelcall.Intend`, refuse a lease with any non-positive reservation (an `ErrNoGroups` sibling). The refusal in `owed` then becomes unreachable for a bound call. Or file it for P22a with a test.

**M3. The projection names are fragile, and adcd's wiring is unguarded** (`ledger/record.go:96-97`, `:110`).
- **Name drift:** M7's tests catch a renamed projection, but only in this package's tests.
  - **Fix:** a compile-time single source. `modelcall` can import `ledger` without a cycle, because `ledger` imports neither `modelcall` nor `gateway`. Define the names once in `ledger`, and have `modelcall.Projection` and `modelcall.LeaseProjection` use them.
- **Missing wiring:** if P22a wires `modelcall.Kinds` and `Intend` but forgets `modelcall.Project` in `daemon/wiring.go`, `BoundTo` returns an empty list. Every settlement then names no calls and passes, so every call is charged zero.
  - **Fix:** a start check, like `CheckHistory`, that refuses a store holding `model_call.intended` events without the binding projections. Or file it for P22a with a test.

### Low

**L1. `liable` has no aggregate bound** (`liability.go:185`; admission at `reserve.go:330`).
- `owed` caps each lease at 2^53−1, but `t.Liable` adds deltas without a cap.
- Probe 4: one hostile delta puts `liable` at 9.0e15, and every later grant on the group is refused until reconciliation. That is fail-closed, but the group stays blocked.
- About 1024 such leases would overflow `int64`, so `held` goes negative and admission opens.
- **Fix:** saturate `Liable` at `maxAmount`. Do not refuse, since a refusal is another wedge. Record the group-wide DoS and its operator unblock path for P20.

**L2. Entry 61's blast radius** (`ledger/record.go:61`; `adapterhost/submit.go:200`, `:256`).
- **Who can trigger it:** the host fills `attempt` and `lease` from its spec, so a hostile adapter cannot forge a mismatch. Only a host or spec bug can.
- **Damage:** in that case, the retry `finish(…, false)` reuses the cached `r.measured` and fails again. The attempt's end, `attempt.lost`, is then never recorded.
- **Fix:** on a binding mismatch, have the retry drop the usage and record a refusal, so the attempt still closes.
- **Not covered:** a report on an unbound call is accepted unchecked, and a later binding is not checked against earlier reports. That is harmless today, but should be noted.

**L3. "One call per attempt" is assumed, not enforced.** `modelcall.Intend` does not refuse a second call on the same attempt. If P22a ever binds two calls on one attempt and both are unmeasured, they share one reservation. That is defensible, since the reservation is sized to the attempt's whole footprint, but it should be asserted in `Intend` or in a test.

**L4. Documentation drift.** The §1 gating-table row (`00-slice-plan.md:48`) still says "`Reservations.release` refuses a settlement with liabilities". The I6 and I9 rows are left for the lead's reconcile.

**L5. Batch-level fixtures are order-dependent.** `fixtures/build.go`, `fixtures/adapterhost.go` and `harness/harness.go` run their projectors over the whole batch, not event by event (`errors.Join(project, ledger.Project)`). A command that appends a measurement and the settlement together would hit `checkSettlement` before the measurement is in the ledger. adcd's `ProjectEach` is safe. Keep settlement in its own command, or compose the fixtures with `ProjectEach`.

### Answers to the brief
- **Charged once (I9):** holds, except H1 (charged zero times, with the lease held forever) and M1 (the charge is whatever the upstream claims).
- **The F case:** sound. A lease has exactly one attempt (`granted → active` once; supersession takes a new lease), so holding the fallback once per lease is safe under supersession.
- **Late delta lowers the fallback:** see M1.
- **Admission:** liabilities count exactly like reservations (`held = reserved + liable`). They cannot go negative, but they can overflow (L1).
- **Settlement refusal:** see H1 and M2.
- **Entry 61:** see L2.
- **Entry 62:** pricing uses tokens only, rounds up, uses `big.Int` and is overflow-safe. The tokens themselves are adapter-reported (M1).
- **Replay:** the folds are deterministic, and a store with no liabilities replays byte for byte. The fold checks the binding's attempt and lease only.
- **Import-cycle workaround:** see M3.
- **Fault evidence:** M1 and M5 reproduce as claimed.
