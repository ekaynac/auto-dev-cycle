## Review of #40 (Astra, 2026-10-04)

Verdict: NO-GO

Line numbers refer to `9e4728d`. The checkout’s subsequent commit adds only an SPDX header.

1. **High — The shared spec omits KA1 disclosure controls.**  
   **Location:** `spec/knowledge/invariants.md:16`, `spec/knowledge/vocabulary.md:68`.  
   Inherited restrictions and human approval for prose are preserved, but §6 also requires framework-only scope, allowlisted telemetry through fixed templates for automatic export, the mechanical content filter, and opaque evidence pointers exposing no cross-project metadata. A second implementation could satisfy these invariants while exposing source-project paths or symbols through evidence pointers.  
   **Fix:** Carry those requirements into the normative vocabulary/invariants, including that approval never overrides inherited restrictions. Define fixture expectations covering pointer metadata and the automatic-versus-prose export paths.

2. **Medium — Three fixtures are deferred past their owning milestone.**  
   **Location:** `spec/knowledge/fixtures/definitions.yaml:32`, `:103`, `:115`.  
   `on-prem-lesson-to-open-project`, `mixed-project-handover`, and `refactor-under-anchored-item` are assigned M4. The plan assigns admission/exposure fixtures to M2 (§6 timing; E4), including mixed-project handovers, and anchored-item validity to M2/E5. M4 owns two-project integration. Delaying these checks also leaves the M2 fixture-lesson experiment without its specified safeguards.  
   **Fix:** Assign these fixture checks to M2; describe M4 integration separately. The M1 checks for existing view, continuity, citation, and coverage behaviour can remain M1.

3. **Medium — The revocation fixture requires outcomes its inputs do not establish.**  
   **Location:** `spec/knowledge/fixtures/definitions.yaml:46`.  
   Revocation “before the call is sent” can occur after the append-only intent already includes the ref, making “the manifest intent never lists it as included” impossible without rewriting history. Revoking one destination also does not establish that another fallback destination must refuse the ref.  
   **Fix:** Specify the ordering relative to intent commitment and explicit destination permissions. Separate pre-intent exclusion from post-intent revocation; preserve the latter’s original intent and record the refused delivery. Require refusal at fallback only when that destination also fails the live checks.

4. **Medium — Supervisor grant isolation is asserted by a fixture but absent from its invariant.**  
   **Location:** `spec/knowledge/vocabulary.md:15`, `spec/knowledge/fixtures/definitions.yaml:102`.  
   The vocabulary omits §3’s requirement that supervisor continuity be untrusted and scoped to the supervisor’s grants and projects. Invariant 4 concerns claim acceptance/export; it does not establish which records a receiving supervisor may read. Consequently, the handover’s filtering requirement does not follow from its named invariant.  
   **Fix:** Restore grant/project scoping, make authorization of referenced records explicit, and bind the handover fixture to that rule.

5. **Medium — The anchored-item fixture invents an unconditional `stale` result.**  
   **Location:** `spec/knowledge/fixtures/definitions.yaml:117`, `spec/knowledge/invariants.md:34`.  
   No declared dependencies are supplied, yet the outcome requires revalidation and specifically `stale`. K6 says a **dependency change** produces `stale` **or** `unknown`; resolving or moving an anchor establishes location, not validity. Invariant 8 currently states neither dependency invalidation nor revalidation.  
   **Fix:** Provide a concrete dependency changed by the refactor, restore K6’s invalidation/revalidation rule, and allow `stale` or `unknown` unless an authoritative rule selects one.

6. **Medium — Coverage fixtures lack a reproducible status derivation.**  
   **Location:** `spec/knowledge/vocabulary.md:92`, `spec/knowledge/fixtures/definitions.yaml:93`.  
   The vocabulary lists `complete`, `partial`, and `unknown` without defining their selection. The declared-only fixture supplies no independently evidenced delivery but mandates `partial`; “derived from evidence” and “unknown stays unknown” do not establish that result. Merely having delivery events in the compaction fixture likewise leaves their evidentiary contents unspecified.  
   **Fix:** State the derivation rules consistently with the manifest contract, including the design note’s complete-coverage condition. Supply the observed deliveries and compaction event needed for each fixture’s exact expected status.

7. **Medium — “Another file, tree or environment” does not establish irrelevant evidence.**  
   **Location:** `spec/knowledge/fixtures/definitions.yaml:70`.  
   Evidence from another file or environment can entail a claim—for example, a claim explicitly comparing environments. The fixture supplies neither the claim’s applicability nor the evidence’s subject, so rejection is not reproducible from its inputs alone.  
   **Fix:** Specify a concrete claim about a particular subject/tree/environment, evidence about an incompatible subject, and the absence of other supporting evidence.

8. **Medium — The vendoring instruction excludes normative dependencies.**  
   **Location:** `spec/knowledge/README.md:22`, `spec/knowledge/schemas/README.md:3`.  
   Vendoring only this directory omits the normative JSON Schemas. Those also depend on files outside `schemas/records/ref.json` and `view-fingerprint.json`, including `common/defs.json` and `records/receipt.json`. InfraMedic cannot resolve the contract from the prescribed vendored content.  
   **Fix:** Require the spec and its complete schema dependency closure at the same pinned repository revision, with a documented layout or resolver mapping. Keep the existing single schema source.

Validation: the knowledge package tests passed through the repository’s test sandbox. An independent Python calculation reproduced every golden preimage and digest. No digest mismatch was found; full `make ci` was not rerun.
