### 2. The L1 guard change: are any verdicts different from main's?

I found none. `r.testUnder[dir]` (`internal/accept/integrity.go:196-201`, `:223`) gives the same result as main's check, "any base `q` with `goByName(q) && HasPrefix(q, dir+"/")`":
- The ancestor walk stops early only at a directory already marked. Every marked directory's ancestors are marked too, so the early stop loses nothing.
- `dir` is never `.`, because `p != "go.mod"`.

To check this, I wrote a differential probe over `guardBase` plus extra base tests:
