For the work directory that is `$TMPDIR` or `/tmp`; for a root, `/tmp`; for its `runs/`, `/dev/shm`. `entry` (`internal/secretscan/scan.go:115-118`) `Lstat`s every entry `ReadDir` listed, and any error, ENOENT included, ends the walk with `ErrIncomplete`.

- **Races in the content walk (O1).**

### 2. The forms
- **The secret forms against the deleted shell `forms`** (5f3b190a's, the r2 hex fix included), run under `LANG=C` as the driver runs it:
  - 6,100 inputs: base64url, `tok_` tokens, and random strings of `A z 0 9 - _ + / = . space tab é \xff ! \r`;
  - every set of forms, and every refusal (a form shorter than 16, or a decode error), is equal, **except** for a secret that ends in CR;
  - `readInput` (`internal/cli/scan.go:164-170`) uses `bufio.ScanLines`, which drops a trailing `\r`. The forms are then those of another secret: the as-is form still matches as a substring, but the base64 and hex forms of the real secret are missed (O2).
  - The driver's own secrets (stub credential, bootstrap secret, token) cannot end in CR.
- **The canary forms against #440's `canaryForms`** (07f2d474:`cycle-expect.mjs`, verbatim), over 3,002 canaries with a double quote, a backslash, `< > &`, U+2028, U+2029, control characters, é, an emoji, CR, LF, BS, FF, U+00A0 and U+FEFF:
  - **all 1,535 without U+FEFF are equal**: as is, JSON-escaped once and twice, Go's `\u003c`/`\u003e`/`\u0026`/`\u2028`/`\u2029` once and twice, both compositions, and the trimmed core;
  - **311 with U+FEFF differ.** JavaScript's `trim()` strips U+FEFF; Go's `strings.TrimSpace` (`forms.go:67`, and `parseJSON`, `scan.go:287`) does not.
  - Go also refuses a trimmed core shorter than 16 (18 inputs) where JavaScript keeps it. That fails closed.
  - **JSON keys:** Go searches a key's text but does not decode it further; JavaScript does (`decoded(k, depth)`).
  - **`--canary` is called by no #443 route.** It is #440's, whose minted canary is ASCII. So this is O3, for #440's adoption.

### Findings
- **B1** (BLOCKING under entry 202: a regression against main, and a host fault recorded as the case's result).
  - **Where:** `internal/secretscan/scan.go:115-118`. The depth-1 name searches over `/tmp` and `/dev/shm` (`scripts/lib/base-remote.sh:64`, `scripts/lib/sandbox-root.sh:196`) end "did not complete" when another process removes any entry between `ReadDir` and `Lstat`.
  - **Effect:** 87 of 200 under churn, against 0 of 200 for the deleted `find`. The hostile cases die, exit 1.
  - **Fix:** in a name search, match the listed name first, and treat ENOENT of a listed entry as gone. Content searches stay strict.
  - **Row:** that churn probe.
- **O1** (OBLIGATION, Low: fails closed, unbounded).
  - **Where:** `holds` (`scan.go:213`) uses plain `os.Open` after `Lstat`; a FIFO swapped in hangs the scan.
  - **Effect:** probed, scan 106 of a swap loop hung. `adc` ignores the first SIGTERM (`cmd/adc` makes it a context; `devScan` never reads it).
  - **Fix:** `O_NOFOLLOW|O_NONBLOCK` plus an `fstat` check for a regular file with the `Lstat`'s inode, as `internal/sandbox/run.go:480` does; end `devScan` on its context.
- **O2** (OBLIGATION, Low).
  - **Where:** `readInput` (`internal/cli/scan.go:164-170`) drops a trailing `\r` from each secret, so the base64 and hex forms of a CR-ending secret are not searched. The other 6,100 fuzzed inputs equal the deleted shell `forms`.
  - **Fix:** split on `\n` only, or refuse a line holding `\r`.
  - **Row:** a secret ending in CR.
- **O3** (OBLIGATION, Low; for #440's use of `--canary`).
  - **Where:** `CanaryForms` (`forms.go:67`) and `parseJSON` (`scan.go:287`) trim with `strings.TrimSpace`, which keeps U+FEFF. JavaScript's `trim()` drops it, so 311 of 3,002 fuzzed canaries differ. Also, JSON keys are not decoded further, as the reference rule decodes them.
  - **Fix:** trim U+FEFF too, and decode keys as values. Or state both differences where #440 replaces its rule.
- **O4** (OBLIGATION, Low: documentation).
  - **Where:** `sandbox-root.sh:226-227`, `secretscan.Search.LinksInside` and the PR body say a sandbox root's links are "recorded". Nothing records them.
  - **Fix:** say they are neither followed nor reported, or report them on a separate stream.
