   [internal/capacity/reserve.go:61](/tmp/claude-1000/-home-mega/d7e41cde-3883-4e5c-8d98-04b890c5f4c4/scratchpad/wt-reserve/internal/capacity/reserve.go:61) accepts `concurrency`, `slots`, `on_settle` groups. Once an attempt starts, expiry/revocation leads to `settling`; `released` is unavailable. The slot therefore cannot return.

   **Verified:** an in-memory test overlay granted one concurrency slot, started and stopped its attempt, revoked the lease, and attempted settlement without liabilities. Settlement failed with “reserves slots, which may owe a restore”; outstanding remained **1**. This affects honest use and can exhaust the group.

   **Fix:** reject slot groups before granting until supported, or give the lease projector authoritative, replay-safe group classification so ordinary slots settle while evidence targets retain their restore guard. Add an integration test through this lifecycle.

2. **Medium — The widened reason lacks a matching release-driven resume contract.**

Settlement/release can free capacity independently of either. Merely widening the reason description leaves the scheduler’s wake-up obligation unspecified.

   **Fix:** preferably introduce a reservation-capacity reason/hold whose concrete signal identifies the blocking group’s capacity revision. Alternatively, explicitly extend `quota_reset` semantics and 02/05 to include reservation releases and define the probe fallback. Nonblocking here because the production waiting scheduler is deferred.

The remaining contract judgments:

- **Atomicity is correct:** capacity checks and writes share the serialized writer transaction. Refusal rolls back events, lease projections, reservations, effects, and command results. Duplicate group references and mismatched capacity units are rejected.

A second grant is refused; an idempotent command repeat bypasses projection. Expiry retains reservations until `released` or `settled`, appropriately.
- **Rebuild is a deterministic fold for admitted histories.** It starts from fresh projections and processes events in order. Skipping current group admission checks is appropriate: historical grants must not be rejudged against changed configuration. New submissions cannot select replay mode. This proves accounting reconstruction, not historical limit compliance.

- **Grant refusal can coexist with `lease.waiting`:** the failed grant correctly leaves the lease requested. M2 must subsequently persist the waiting transition, handle intervening capacity changes, and recover after interruption. Document that responsibility in 03/05.
- **The fixture exercises real writer contention with deterministic assertions.** Sorting outcomes avoids depending on winner identity; subsequent grants verify capacity reuse. The mutation realistically models checking the limit while forgetting outstanding reservations.

1. **Medium — Projector composition makes acceptance depend on event batching.**  
   `internal/fixtures/capacity.go:45` runs the entire lease batch before capacity projection; `internal/lease/wiring.go:404` therefore cannot see a grant earlier in that batch.

   **Verified:** an in-memory probe submitted grant → start → stop → revoke → settle together. Settlement was refused as potentially owing a restore. The identical events submitted individually succeeded and rebuilt without drift. This conflicts with `Hooks.Project`’s batch-versus-single-event contract.

   **Fix:** compose projectors **per event**, running lease then capacity for each event in order. Apply that composition consistently and add a batch-equivalence regression test.

2. **Medium — Rebuild manufactures eligibility for grants predating capacity wiring.**  
   `internal/capacity/reserve.go:146` skips admission during replay, but line 153 unconditionally creates `capacity_granted`. The log contains no evidence that this projector originally admitted that grant.

   **Verified:** a grant recorded without capacity had `RestoreFree=false` live and `RestoreFree=true` during rebuild, producing two projection differences. This could misclassify an old evidence-target reservation.

   **Fix:** record historical admission/classification provenance, or enforce the documented “wired from the first grant” restriction by refusing incompatible histories. Do not infer eligibility merely from encountering `lease.granted`.

   This is nonblocking here: current rebuild only compares temporary projections and rolls back; the live legacy lease remains guarded. I found no current settlement bypass through that operation.

   `docs/design/m0/05-leases-attempts-fencing.md:120` includes `quota_unknown`, then says the scheduler waits on `capacity_released`. The registry correctly maps unknown quota to `quota_reset`.

   **Fix:** explicitly distinguish the two reason-to-hold mappings.
