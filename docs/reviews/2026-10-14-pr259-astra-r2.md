## External review of #259, round 2 (Astra, 2026-10-14)

Verdict: GO with changes

No Critical or High findings. H1 is closed for this process’s gateway requests. The accepted restart residual remains.

1. **Low — Uncounted floors retain Clock entries indefinitely.**  
   **Location:** `internal/modelcall/clock.go:70`, `internal/modelcall/clock.go:87`.

   A successful floor whose response never produces a committed count remains in `at` for the process’s lifetime, even after its grace expires and reconciliation completes. Repeated unreadable or interrupted responses therefore accumulate entries. The PR acknowledges this accurately.

   **Fix:** add periodic or opportunistic eviction of committed entries whose monotonic age exceeds `FloorGrace` and whose pending count is zero. Evict under the same mutex, and cover expiry while another submission remains pending. Cleanup solely inside `young` is insufficient: reconciler short-circuiting can prevent that method from being called.

2. **Low — The deadline comment promises more than the implementation bounds, and its fixture checks only the initial context.**  
   **Location:** `internal/gateway/gateway.go:207`, `internal/gateway/count_test.go:307`.

   “The whole request, its floor’s commit included, ends within RequestDeadline” is inaccurate. Floor submission uses `WithoutCancel` at line 464; a writer wait can exceed the deadline. A blocked downstream write can also keep `ServeHTTP` alive. The actual guarantee is sufficient: the **outbound request retains the deadline established before floor submission**.

   The new test observes a lease-read deadline, but would still pass if outbound request construction subsequently discarded it.

   **Fix:** narrow the comment to the outbound request’s lifetime. Extend the fixture to inspect the outgoing context, release a blocked floor submission after cancellation and verify no send, and exercise cancellation during an SSE read with the client timeout disabled or longer.
