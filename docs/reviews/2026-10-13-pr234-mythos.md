If the builder defines `TestIssue170NoVersion`, `scope170` or any of the four names, the overlaid package does not build and the gate fails for a reason unrelated to the requirement. The names are published in the builder's clone and the attachment name invites that prefix. Separately, the hidden test calls the visible helpers `adc` and `fake` and the constants `human` and `review` from `cli_test.go`. Replace `acceptance.at_base` in the in-repo `baseline.json` with counts and keep the names in the lead's store. Re-record both digests.
