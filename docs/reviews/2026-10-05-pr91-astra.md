# PR #91 (B3, cited tests): Astra reviews

## Review of #91 (Astra, 2026-10-05)

Verdict: NO-GO

- **High — Collection does not establish execution or success.** `docs/design/m0/06-receipts.md:123`; `docs/plan/01-first-release-plan.md:334`. The operative rule rejects absent and failed IDs but leaves collected, skipped tests unspecified. A pre-existing conditional skip can produce a readable per-test entry without executing assertions; it need not trigger the integrity guard against newly introduced skips. A membership-plus-no-failure implementation could therefore pass an unsupported claim under ordinary use. **Fix:** require an explicit completed, passing result for every cited test. Define skipped, collection-only and incomplete execution as non-passing (`could_not_run` when execution did not complete). Preserve rejection of failed tests even when the overall baseline-relative gate passes.

- **Medium — Test identity and environment coverage need an explicit matching contract.** `docs/design/m0/06-receipts.md:124`; `docs/plan/01-first-release-plan.md:335`. Rule 2 correctly prevents borrowing a receipt from another tree, environment, contract revision or demand. However, neither this addition nor the result-adapter contract defines test-ID identity: duplicate names across files/packages and parametrised cases can be conflated. The singular “applicable gate receipt” also leaves the citation’s required environment coverage implicit. **Fix:** define adapter-produced, fully qualified case IDs that retain file/package/suite identity and parameter identity; require exact matching and reject ambiguous identities. State which declared environments each citation must satisfy, and forbid satisfying one environment’s obligation with another’s result. Distinguish concrete citations from §13’s producer test-ID patterns.

- **Medium — Specify the outcome and recovery path for unreadable results.** `docs/design/m0/06-receipts.md:125`; `docs/plan/01-first-release-plan.md:336`. Holds **are** a registered concept (`02-vocabulary.md:60`), but “hold” is not a receipt outcome or a particular registered hold reason. Vocabulary §02 already classifies unreadable output as `could_not_run`. **Fix:** retain that outcome and name the recovery routing—for example, `runner_fault` → `wrong_owner`, with the responsible owner and concrete resume signal. Distinguish this from structural waiting for a missing gate input (§13). The uncollected → `could_not_run` and failed-test → blocking failure decisions are otherwise appropriate.

- **Medium — The fixture proves only the simplest membership check.** `docs/design/m0/11-testing.md:173`. Missing-ID and passing-ID cases would both pass an implementation containing the skipped-test loophole. They do not exercise acceptance rows, failed/unreadable results, identity collisions, or environment applicability. **Fix:** expand the fixture matrix to cover those cases, parametrised IDs, and strict options both off and on. Assert actual derived outcomes and recovery signals. Include refreshed gate evidence with a reused/carried review, so §13’s reuse cannot preserve a pass after its cited evidence stops qualifying. The positive case should establish eligibility of the test evidence; semantic claim acceptance still requires review.

The revised strict option is consistent: it makes citation mandatory, while validation is unconditional. The byte-identical statement concerns **packet, schema and prompt**, so a controller-side check need not contradict it. M5’s row also remains consistent because mandatory `test_ids` is still a strict option.

Reviewed the requested surrounding contracts and `git diff HEAD~1`. `git diff --check HEAD~1` passes. No files modified.
### Lead notes on round 1 (applied)

- **High (collection is not execution):** a cited test now needs a completed, passing result. Absent, skipped, collection-only and incomplete results are `could_not_run`. A failed result fails the row, even when the baseline-relative gate passes. The plan step is renamed "cited tests ran and passed".
- **Medium (identity and environments):**
  - Citations name concrete cases by the result adapter's fully qualified id (file or package, suite, case, parameters) and match exactly.
  - A citation that matches no case, or more than one, is `could_not_run`.
  - A citation must hold in every gate environment the claim covers (by default every declared environment), and one environment's result never satisfies another's.
  - Concrete citations are distinguished from a producer's test-id patterns (13).
- **Medium (unreadable results):** the outcome is `could_not_run/runner_fault`, on the `wrong_owner` hold for the runner's owner. This is distinct from 13's structural wait for a gate result that does not exist yet.
- **Medium (fixture):** the fixture is now a matrix, run with the strict options off and on, for both a claim and an acceptance row. It covers:
  - uncollected;
  - skipped, collection-only and incomplete;
  - failed while the gate passes;
  - unreadable;
  - an ambiguous name, and a parametrised case cited without its parameters;
  - a citation present in one environment but absent in another;
  - a carried review losing its pass on refreshed receipts;
  - an eligible positive case, which still needs its review.
- **Reuse:** a reused or carried review keeps its pass only while its cited evidence still qualifies on the refreshed prospective-tree receipts.

## Second review of #91 (Astra, 2026-10-05)

Verdict: GO with changes

No remaining Critical/High finding under D43. The remaining issues concern adapter normalization, reason mapping, and fixture completeness.

- **Round-1 High — Collection versus execution: resolved.** `docs/design/m0/06-receipts.md:133` requires completed, passing results, rejects skipped/collection-only/incomplete results, and blocks failed citations even when the baseline-relative gate passes.
- **Round-1 Medium — Identity and environments: resolved at the matching-rule level.** `06-receipts.md:127` requires exact, fully qualified concrete case IDs, including parameters. Coverage defaults to every declared environment. Producer patterns in §13 cannot substitute for concrete citations; adapter capability remains an issue below.
- **Round-1 Medium — Unreadable-result routing: resolved.** `06-receipts.md:136` specifies `could_not_run/runner_fault → wrong_owner`, names the runner’s owner, and distinguishes missing-input structural waits. §02 supplies the `human_act` resume signal and requires its concrete instance.
- **Round-1 Medium — Fixture: substantially resolved.** `docs/design/m0/11-testing.md:173` now covers both row types, both strict-option settings, negative outcomes, identity, environments, and refreshed evidence after carry-over. Recovery assertions remain incomplete below.

**New findings, by severity**

1. **Medium — Expected failures and retry aggregation lack an explicit normalization rule.** `docs/design/m0/06-receipts.md:133`.

   “Completed, passing” excludes an actual failure, but the contract does not specify whether an adapter may normalize an expected failure into success, or expose only the successful final attempt of a failed-then-passed case. Exact case identity alone does not define how multiple attempts combine. This leaves adapter-dependent interpretations of the new rule.

   **Fix:** distinguish ordinary pass, expected failure, unexpected pass, and retry history. Expected failure must not establish the asserted behavior. Define pass-on-retry eligibility explicitly; the conservative interpretation of the existing failed-result rule is that an observed failure within the applicable gate run blocks the citation. Missing required attempt/status information should yield `could_not_run`. Add expected-failure and fail→pass fixtures.

2. **Medium — Partially informative adapters need an explicit fail-closed capability rule.** `docs/design/m0/06-receipts.md:127`; `docs/design/m0/07-project-profile.md:49`.

   The plan already handles `none`: without per-test results, per-test evidence is `could_not_run` (`01-first-release-plan.md:288`). The uncovered boundary is an adapter that emits readable case-like records but cannot preserve parameter identity or distinguish skipped execution from a pass. Merely finding one matching record does not establish that its identity and execution status are complete.

   **Fix:** require adapters to establish every fact needed for citation eligibility. Unknown parameters, execution status, or case granularity must yield `could_not_run`; do not synthesize missing facts from aggregate success. Apply this by capability, rather than banning TAP categorically. Add fixtures for `none`, lost parameter identity, and unreportable skips.

3. **Medium — The new outcomes need a reason-code mapping, especially baseline-known failures.** `docs/design/m0/06-receipts.md:134`; `schemas/vocab/reasons.yaml:35`.

   The registered `gate_test_failed` means failure **relative to the measured baseline** and permits only `found`. It does not describe the newly required rejection of a cited test whose failure is already in the baseline. Likewise, absent/skipped/ambiguous citations are assigned outcomes without reasons or recovery routing. Treating all of them as `runner_fault` would incorrectly send citation defects to the runner’s owner.

   **Fix:** specify the `(outcome, reason, recovery)` mapping now, including whether “fails the row” means a blocking `found` predicate or `failed`. Introduce a citation-specific reason where existing meanings do not fit, and register it before any writer or executable fixture emits it. Unreadable results already have a suitable code; no new hold enum is needed.

4. **Low — Recovery and semantic-boundary fixtures remain incomplete.** `docs/design/m0/11-testing.md:177`; `docs/plan/01-first-release-plan.md:339`.

   The unreadable-result fixture checks entry into `wrong_owner`, but does not require the concrete resume signal or demonstrate recovery. Also, “a placeholder has nothing to pass on” overstates the mechanical check: an empty test body can complete successfully.

   **Fix:** assert the named owner, concrete `human_act` signal, and recovery through fresh qualifying evidence; separately test structural waiting before a gate result exists. Add a passing no-op case whose claim is rejected by semantic review, and describe mechanical success as evidence eligibility.

A zero-assertion result is **not independently a blocker here**: plan §2.6 still requires inspection of assertions/mocks and identification of a wrong implementation the evidence catches. Assertion count alone is not a sufficient semantic test. Whole-file/suite citations are excluded by the concrete-case rule. Environment exclusions are not implicit: the default covers every declared environment, and narrower claims remain subject to requirement reconciliation and approved contract scope. Rule 8a’s refresh requirement also closes the carry-over bypass despite unchanged aggregate gate conclusions.

Both requested diffs pass `git diff --check`. No files modified.
### Lead notes on round 2 (applied)

- **Medium 1 (expected failures and retries):**
  - An expected failure (`xfail`), and an unexpected pass of one, are `could_not_run`.
  - A case that failed on any attempt in the gate run, pass-on-retry included, is a blocking finding.
  - Attempt history is a fact the adapter must report.
- **Medium 2 (adapter capability):** the adapter must establish the case's full identity, whether it executed, and every attempt's status. A missing fact is never synthesized from aggregate success: the citation is `could_not_run`. The rule applies by capability, not by adapter name.
- **Medium 3 (reason mapping):** each case now has an `outcome/reason → recovery` triple:
  - `could_not_run/citation_unqualified` → candidate repair;
  - `found/cited_test_failed` (baseline-known failures included) → candidate repair;
  - `could_not_run/runner_fault` on `wrong_owner` → the runner owner's act, then fresh results re-evaluate the citation.

  The two citation reasons are registered with the gate's implementation (M5), before anything emits them.
- **Low 4:** the fixture asserts the hold's owner and signal, and recovery through fresh results. It also covers:
  - the structural wait before a gate result exists;
  - `xfail`, fail-then-pass on retry, and an adapter that cannot report parameters or skips;
  - a passing empty test, whose claim the review refuses.

  The plan sentence about placeholders is replaced: the check establishes evidence eligibility, and proof stays the review's job.
