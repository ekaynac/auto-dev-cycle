### Low

- **Input:** the row at `internal/bench/bench_test.go:411`: a `final` of 2000/10 tokens at 0 micros labelled `free`, a gateway count of 2000/10, on a priced route. Recorded as `charge {free USD 2100, 1 call}`.
- **Why it is only Low:** the amount is right, and `Compare` keys per basis and currency, so a relabelled call shows as growth under the new key, never as a fall. But the record now says a `free` (or `subscription_equivalent`) call cost 2100 USD, which is the registry's cash valuation, not a free or subscription figure. Round 4 fixed the same label problem for unsettled calls (every unsettled floor is under `cash`); settled calls were left out.
- **Fix:** when `priced > reported`, record the settled charge under `cash` (the floor is a cash valuation), as the `default` arm already does; otherwise keep the reported basis. Change the expected basis in the two rows at `bench_test.go:407-413` to `cash`. Or state in §2.8 that a floored settled call keeps its reported label.

**L2. The liability instruction to `adc dev bench record` can over-record a lease with several uncertain calls.** `internal/bench/bench.go:25-30`, `docs/design/m1/00-slice-plan.md:530`, PR body "Residual, as of round 4"
- **What the ledger returns:** `capacity.owed` (`internal/capacity/liability.go:211-225`) is one amount per lease and group, over all the lease's uncertain calls together (the reservation, or the measured sum if larger). `LiabilitiesOf` returns that per-lease amount.
- **Input:** a settled lease with two crashed attempts' calls, both unmeasured, and a `usd_micros` liability of 5000. Following the doc, the command passes 5000 as each call's `Liability`: `outstanding {cash USD 10000, 2 calls}` for a lease that owes 5000.
- **Why it is only Low:** it over-records and never under-records, and the command is not in this PR. But the doc is the only instruction that command will get.
- **Fix:** in the package doc, §2.8 and the body, say that a lease's `usd_micros` amount is passed once (on one of its unmeasured or unsettled calls, zero on the others), or split across them, never repeated per call.
