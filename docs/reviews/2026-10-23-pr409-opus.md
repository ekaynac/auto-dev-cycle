### 2. Are the two survivors truly equivalent? Yes, both.
- **6a-2** (`Intend`'s lease check): `running` already requires the attempt to be current, `running`, on that lease and that unit. A running attempt has open publication, and `lease/wiring.go:722` refuses any lease leaving `active` while its attempt still publishes. `StartAttempt`/`Supersede` set the lease's attempt. So no reachable state passes `running` and fails the lease check.
- **16a-1** (`usdRail`'s comparison): its only caller is `Match` (`dispatch.go:146`), which passes the same `spend` read that `reservation` sized the lease from. For a priced executor:
  - The reservation is `min(fit, attempt_cap)`.
  - `fit = ⌊left·10⁶ / maxRate⌋`, where `left` is the smaller amount the two rails leave.
  - `PriceTokens(fit)` rounds up, but to at most `left`, because the integer `left` is an upper bound.
  - So `spent + reserved ≤ cap` holds for both rails. A fit below one turn is refused first.
  - At price 0 the reservation costs 0.
  
  The comparison is defence in depth.

### Medium

**M1 (OBLIGATION). adcd never reconciles an `uncertain` `model_call` effect after the attempt's end, and the inventory's I6/I9 effect text says it settles them.**
- `Reconciler.Reconcile` (`modelcall/reconcile.go:99`) has **no production caller** (searched `internal/daemon`, `internal/reconcile` and `internal/recovery`). `Ended` runs once per attempt, at its end.
- `egress.open`'s promise that "a later end retries a settlement that failed" is never exercised: the runner calls `end` once (`run.go:273-276`, the `ended` flag).
- So two cases are never revisited:
  - a settle that failed at the attempt's end leaves the effect `pending` until the next start;
  - every effect that `MarkPendingUncertain` makes `uncertain` at a start stays `uncertain` for good, even when its call's standing has settled.
- **Bounded and visible:**
  - the charge reads the ledger standing, not the effect state (`dispatch.Cost`);
  - liabilities are kept, never released at zero;
  - an `uncertain` `model_call` effect blocks nothing: the only readers of live effects in `graph` are merge's.
- But the effect log will not show settled calls as settled, and once a production `Query.Usage` lands, nothing would ever ask it for a restart-cut call. That is the GAP to keep open for #170's accounting check (assertion 7).
- **Fix:** in the activation PR (or now), run `Reconcile` over the `uncertain`/`pending` `model_call` effects of ended attempts, at start after recovery and on the loop's timer. Or state in I6/I9 effect that only the attempt's end settles, and restart-left effects stay `uncertain` until a `Query.Usage` and its driver land.
