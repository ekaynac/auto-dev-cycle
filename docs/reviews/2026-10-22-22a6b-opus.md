**M2 (OBLIGATION; must close before 22a-6c activates the reviewer). Accepted candidates that cannot get a review context.** `intent.Review` errors in two cases a candidate can cause, and nothing turns either into a verdict:
- **A patch over `review.MaxDiff`** (512 KiB). Accept admits patches up to 64 MiB (`maxPatch`), but `include(v[0], review.MaxDiff)` (`internal/intent/review.go:95`) refuses anything larger.
- **An oversized gate report.** Measured: `json.Marshal` escapes `<`, so one finding whose id is 256 `<` takes 1,590 bytes, and 1,000 such findings take 1.59 MB. That is past `CheckReport`'s own bound (1,217,536 bytes) and past the 1 MiB that `include(v[1], maxSafeBytes)` reads (`review.go:101`). Go subtest names keep `<`, and the test names are the candidate's.

So a reviewer intent cannot be built. Through 22a-6c's runner that would be a pre-launch failure, held `runner: failed`: a candidate's fault recorded as the controller's, and the task stuck. That is High by the rubric once it is reachable.
- **Fix:**
  - 22a-6c records a review verdict for both cases, for example `failed/unsafe_output` with the bound named.
  - The gate's `Report` should bound its bytes after escaping (truncate findings by encoded size, or marshal with `SetEscapeHTML(false)`).
  - Read the report at the gate's own bound, `maxReportBytes`.

**M3 (OBLIGATION; rubric "Reuse"). `readAtMost` is a second bounded, digest-checked blob reader in the same package** (`review.go:132`). `intent.readBlob` (`intent.go:336`) already reads at most `maxSafeBytes` and checks the digest. I searched `internal/intent` and `internal/daemon` for readers: there is also `runner.readBlob`. The builder's `Projector.prompt` reads the brief with **no** digest check (`recorded.go`, unchanged). So the rule "read a blob bounded and check it is its digest's" now has three implementations, and one path skips half of it.
- **Fix:** give `readBlob` a limit parameter, and use it in `Review` and `prompt`.
