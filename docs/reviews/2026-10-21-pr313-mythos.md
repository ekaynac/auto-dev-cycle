## External review of #313, round 2 (Mythos, 2026-10-21)

Verdict: NO-GO

One High: round 3's settled-call floor is bypassed by the adapter-reported `cost.basis`, which the recorder trusts and the residual does not name. It is a one-branch fix. Everything else from Astra's H1–H4 and M5 and Opus's M1–M3 is closed for its inputs and nearest variants.

### High

**H1. A settled call is charged 0 when its adapter labels its basis non-cash.** `internal/bench/bench.go:425`
- **Input:** Opus's M1 input 1 with one field changed. An adapter `final` of 2000/10 tokens at 0 micros with `cost.basis: "free"` (or `subscription_equivalent`), plus a `gateway_count` of 2000/10. The gateway stamps counts `cash`/USD (`internal/modelcall/modelcall.go:411`), so `basisOf` takes the adapter's `free`. `StandingOf` settles the call because the final covers the count. The first `switch` arm fires and the call is charged `{free USD 0, 1 call}`. The identical call with basis `cash` is charged 2100.
- **Why it is in the promise:** §2.8 (`docs/design/m1/00-slice-plan.md:524`) and the schema now say a settled call is charged no less than its registry price because "the ledger never trusts a reported cost". The basis arrives in the same adapter-written block as that cost (`internal/adapterhost/submit.go:305-330`, `u.Cost` and `f.Cost` pass through verbatim). So the adapter picks whether the floor applies. The recorder cannot check it: `Meta` carries rates only, not the route's registry `cost_basis`. The row at `internal/bench/bench_test.go:394` pins the bypass as intended.
- **Fix, either:** drop the `basis != "cash"` exemption and floor every settled call at `PriceMicros` of its standing figure. Free and local routes already must declare a zero price, so the floor is nil there, and a subscription-equivalent route's price is the equivalent. Refuse a non-USD settled call only when the floor is positive. Or carry each route's registry `cost_basis` in `Meta` and refuse a settled call whose reported basis is not its route's. Change the row at `bench_test.go:394` (0 → 2100, or a refusal), add Opus's input 1 with basis `free`, and restate §2.8.
