### Medium

**M1. A settled call can be charged 0 while its tokens are positive, and its charge can fall below what the same call owed while unsettled.**
- **Where:** `internal/bench/bench.go:369-370`. A settled call is charged its reported `CostMicros`, with no floor.
- **Input 1:** an adapter `final` of 2000/10 tokens at 0 micros, plus a `gateway_count` of 2000/10. Seen: settled, tokens 2000/10, `charge {cash USD 0, 1 call}`.
- **Input 2:** a `provider_reconciliation` of 0/0 at 0 micros, below a gateway count of 2000/10. The standing is Settled and Below. Seen: tokens raised to 2000/10, charge 0.
- **Why it matters:** the same call is outstanding at no less than its registry price while unsettled, then drops to 0 once it settles. The ledger's own rule is that pricing never reads `CostMicros`, because "an upstream can report any cost, zero included" (`ledger.go`, `PriceMicros`, #226 entry 62). Charging the reported cost does follow §2.8's literal "Σ `cost.amount`", so this is not a blocker.
- **Fix:** do one of these:
  - floor a settled `cash` call at `PriceMicros` of its standing figure, as unsettled calls already are;
  - or keep the reported cost and also record the registry price, and state the choice in §2.8 and the package doc.

  Add a row for each of the two inputs above.

**M2. The residual does not cover everything the recorder trusts.**
- **Where:** the PR body's "Residual" section, and `bench.go:119-126` and `30-33`. They name only `Liability` as trusted. Also trusted:
  - **That the inventory is complete.** A bound call with no measurement that the caller leaves out of `Task.Calls` disappears with no trace. This is Astra's H2 case once more, now with the caller as the gap.
  - **`Call.Attempt`.** For an unmeasured call it alone decides the stage.
  - **`Meta.Prices`.** A zero price removes the floor.
- **What the liability really is:** `capacity.LiabilitiesOf` (`internal/capacity/liability.go:260-277`) returns an amount per lease and resource group, in the group's unit. It exists only after the lease has settled (`owe`, `liability.go:150`), and it is deleted when it reaches 0. So "the call's share in USD micros" holds only if the caller picks a `usd_micros` group, and only once the lease has settled.
- **Fix:** list each of these as trusted. State that `adc dev bench record` must:
  - pass every call `ledger.BoundTo` returns for each lease of the task;
  - pass the `usd_micros` group's amount;
  - pass the reservation itself for a lease that has not settled yet.

**M3. The stated false blocks miss some cases, and one is wider than it reads.**
- **An unclaimed node.** "A node with no claimed time" is refused (`bench.go:250-251`). A held task can have nodes that were planned and never claimed, for example §2.3's `admission-held` stub case. One such node refuses the whole run, unless the caller leaves it out, and nothing says who drops it.
  - Fix: either count unclaimed nodes apart (as `unfinished`, or a new `pending` count), or state in the doc and the false-block list that the caller leaves them out.
- **No liability.** "A bound call with no measurement and no ledger liability is refused" sounds rare. It is not: by M2, every crashed attempt whose lease has not settled hits it, as does every route without a `usd_micros` group, and the same holds for a zero-figure unsettled call on a free or local route (`Prices` zero). Name these cases.
- **Unlisted refusals from round 1:**
  - a call reported in two bases (for example an adapter `subscription_equivalent` and a provider reconciliation `cash`);
  - a route without a price, which free and local routes must now declare.

### Low

- **L1. Wall time saturates without an error.** `bench.go:229` and `324` call `time.Sub(...).Milliseconds()`, which caps at about 292 years.
  - Input: `Approved` or a node's `Claimed` set to `0001-01-01T00:00:01Z`, which is not zero, so the zero check passes.
  - Seen: task `wall_ms=9223372036854` and gate `wall_ms=9223372036854`, both accepted and both valid against the schema.
  - Fix: refuse when the difference reaches the maximum duration, or refuse any time after `RecordedAt`.
- **L2. A negative cost on a settled final becomes a charge of 0 without an error.** `bench.go:368`: `Reconcile` takes the larger of the final and the delta sum, which is 0.
  - Input: final `cost.amount_micros = -1`. Seen: build charge 1500 for 2 calls, where that call contributes 0.
  - Inputs read over the API are already valid against their schema. Still, decoding `Usage` through `contract.Decode` (or checking for negatives) would make the refusal explicit.
- **L3. One executor per node.** `Node.Executor` assumes all of a node's attempts ran on one executor. A replacement attempt on another route cannot be expressed, and the call would be priced at the first executor's price. Either state that a node's attempts share one executor, or move `Executor` onto each attempt.
