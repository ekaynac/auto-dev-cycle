## External review of #291 (Astra, 2026-10-20)

Verdict: GO with changes

1. **Medium — Unleased result history can disappear from contributors.** `internal/graph/contributors.go:81`

   `State.Result` identifies the **selected** result, not whether a result was ever recorded. Recording a `could_not_run` result leaves it nil; invalidation clears it while preserving the recorded result (`internal/graph/lifecycle.go:198`, `:309`).

   **Concrete input:** In one task, commit an unleased build’s `partial_patch/could_not_run`, then complete another build leased to `gpt`. A `claude` review of the latter can claim and commit GO: contributors contain only `gpt`, although the promised rule requires `unknown` too. Likewise, invalidating an unleased completed build removes its unknown contribution.

   **Fix:** Determine “has a recorded result” from `ResultProjection`, or maintain a persistent per-node marker. Cover unselected results and invalidation at both claim and commit. Opus M1’s original selected-result case is fixed; these nearest variants remain.

2. **Medium — Repair does not bind findings to the candidate actually reviewed.** `internal/graph/repair.go:31`

   `takesBuild` checks the declared producer, while the subsequent loop independently selects the review and the build’s current patch. Neither compares that patch with the result fixed by the review’s claim.

   **Concrete input:** Build B selects P1; review R consumes P1 and commits no_go. Invalidate B and complete it with P2. `Repair(B, R)` accepts R’s findings about P1 alongside `candidate-1` resolving to P2. The review remains completed; `selectedOf` does not recursively revalidate its inputs.

   **Fix:** Require the review’s claimed build input to equal B’s currently selected patch. Add the P1 → review → invalidate → P2 case, expecting refusal until P2 receives its own review.

3. **Low — Opus L2’s diagnostic fix is lost in the evaluator path.** `internal/graph/evaluate.go:290`

   **Concrete input:** An unregistered builder contributes, and a correctly registered reviewer R is evaluated. `ErrNotIndependent.Cause` correctly identifies the unknown contributor, but the evaluator discards it and records only `reviewer_unavailable`, signalled by `registry:R`. The durable hold still points at the innocent reviewer.

   **Fix:** Preserve the refusal cause alongside the hold and distinguish unknown-contributor failures from reviewer-registration failures. Verify the durable diagnostic, not only the returned error.
