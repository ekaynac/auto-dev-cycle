### Answers to the five questions

**1. The label matches what the image warms, and the archive neither leaks nor stays.**
- **The label:** its lockfile hashes (`manifest`) and the build context's `lock/` (`context`) are read from the same `$repo/go.mod` and `$repo/go.sum`, the archive's, in the same run. The Containerfile then downloads and verifies exactly `lock/` (`COPY lock/ warm.txt`, `go mod download`, `go mod verify`). They cannot differ.
- **The sandbox's check:** the sandbox compares the label with the task base's real lockfiles (`CheckManifest`). Even an odd archive, for example an `export-subst` attribute, could only make it refuse, never accept.
- **No leak:** the archive lies in its own `mktemp -d`, apart from the build context. Only the two lockfiles and `warm.txt` (derived from the archive by `go list`) enter the context.
- **No leftover:** `rmbase` runs on every exit: the `manifest` and `context` traps, and the build's `cleanup`. Probe 1 shows it.
