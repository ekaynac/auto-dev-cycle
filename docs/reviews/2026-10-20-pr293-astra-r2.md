2. **Medium — The cumulative wire budget rejects honest listing traffic within main’s limits.**  
   `internal/accept/integrity_proc.go:179`; `internal/accept/integrity_proc.go:547`

   **Fix:** deduplicate module payloads across listing requests, or derive the cumulative protocol allowance from the full legal request envelope, including space for the terminal result. Cover repeated identical blobs across different module roots.
