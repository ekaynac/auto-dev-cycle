### Medium

**M1. The fold does not bind the intent to the build's actual inputs, lease or call** (`internal/intent/recorded.go:70-81`). I recorded each of these at a real launch through the `recordIntent` seam, and **every one was accepted, with the attempt started**:
- **no inputs at all** (inputs, included and omitted all empty), while the builder still received the brief;
- the brief **omitted** (`budget`) rather than included, while it was rendered and sent;
- another `consumer.executor`, another `call`, another `rendered_digest`, another `policy.data_policy_digest`, `routing: reported`, and `budget.tokens` of 2^40.

The design names its forged-intent rows (a non-brief input, the brief with fewer restrictions or another scope) and those are refused. But 10's invariant is that controller-supplied context comes only from declared inputs, and that the manifest lists exactly those. A recorded intent that lists nothing while the brief was sent breaks that, and neither replay nor `verify-log` would notice.
- **Fix:** in the fold, bind what the log already holds:
  - `inputs` and `included` equal the node's rendered context at its claim (`graph.Context`);
  - `consumer.executor` equals the attempt's lease's executor;
  - `policy.data_policy_digest` equals the restriction's `policy_digest`;
  - `budget.tokens` is at most the lease's token reservation.
  
  Add a row for each.

**M2. A restricted ref is not checked against the intent's destination.** **Probe:** `destination: "elsewhere"`, which is outside the brief's `allowed_destinations: [stub]`, was accepted. 10 says: "Restricted refs are checked at intent … against … the receiving project's policy and destination." Today Match's routing check stops this before launch, so the gap is in the defense in depth only.
- **Fix:** in the fold (or in `intent.Check`), refuse any listed ref whose restrictions do not allow `mi.Destination`, or whose routing is `verified` while the intent's is `reported`. Add a row.

### Low

**L1. The sizes do not agree** (`internal/daemon/run.go:336`, `:358`).
- `included[0].bytes` is the brief blob's size (278), not the bytes it renders into the prompt (7).
- `budget.bytes` is the prompt's own length, which makes it a measure of what was used, not a limit.
- So the included bytes exceed the budget, and any later check that the included bytes fit the budget would refuse the run falsely.
- **Fix:** record the rendered size for the included ref, and set `budget.bytes` from a real limit (for example the brief's maximum, or the executor's context window), or state the meaning.

**L2. `routing` comes from the profile's permission, not from the destination** (`run.go:321`). Under `reported_ok`, the intent says `reported` even when the registry destination is `verification: verified`. That understates; it is not unsafe.
- **Fix:** derive it from `pl.executor.Destination.Verification`.

**L3. Two consumer fields hold other things than their names say** (`run.go:334-335`).
- `harness_version: "http-chat"` is the harness kind, not a version. Since 5c-2a-w the adapter is adcd's own release's, so a real version is known at launch (for example `http-chat@<buildinfo version>`).
- `capability_profile` holds the family, which is review independence's notion, not a capability profile.
- Nothing reads either field today, so this is honest only because the PR body states it.
- **Fix:** record the release version. Name the capability profile after what limits the builder (the edit-loop tool set), or state the stand-in in 10 and in the inventory.

**L4. Replay now reads blobs for every intent event.** `CheckRecorded` runs `intent.Load`, which reads the brief blob and any declassified blob. So a missing or damaged blob makes the startup replay refuse (`ambiguous_state`), and adcd will not start. The seal fold already depends on blobs the same way, so this has precedent.
- **Fix:** state it in the PR body.
